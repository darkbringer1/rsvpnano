# RSVP Nano AMOLED v0.1.1

Bug-fix release: stops the touch driver from spamming false recovery attempts
while idle, and fixes the installer's serial monitor grabbing the wrong port.

## What's Changed

### Touch
- The touch controller (FT3168) goes into a low-power mode while idle and stops
  answering until you touch it again — which is normal. The driver used to
  misread this as a hung bus and constantly tried to "recover" it, flooding the
  log and briefly disrupting the shared power-management chip on every attempt.
  Touch still worked, but the churn was wasteful. It now stays quiet while idle
  and only attempts a real recovery if the bus is genuinely stuck for 30+
  seconds.

### Installer (dev.sh)
- The serial monitor no longer falls back to a stray port (e.g.
  `Bluetooth-Incoming-Port`) when no board is detected. It now reports that no
  board was found and tells you to replug and rescan, instead of opening a dead
  monitor with no error.

## Assets

- `rsvp-nano-amoled.bin` is the full browser-flasher image for fresh installs.
- `rsvp-nano-amoled-ota.bin` is the app-only binary used by OTA updates.
