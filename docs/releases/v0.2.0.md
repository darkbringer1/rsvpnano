# RSVP Nano AMOLED v0.2.0

Adds a deep power-saving standby you trigger with the power button.

## What's Changed

### Power saving (new)
- **Tap the PWR button to put the device into deep standby**: the screen and
  touch turn off for maximum battery saving. Tap PWR again to wake. The BOOT
  button does nothing while in standby, and holding PWR still powers the device
  off as before.
- The device also enters deep standby on its own after a period of inactivity.
- New setting **Settings > Battery > "Standby after"** to choose the idle delay
  (Off / 1 / 3 / 5 / 10 minutes; default 5).
- This is separate from, and deeper than, the existing idle screensaver — it
  cuts the display and touch entirely rather than showing an animation.

## Assets

- `rsvp-nano-amoled.bin` is the full browser-flasher image for fresh installs.
- `rsvp-nano-amoled-ota.bin` is the app-only binary used by OTA updates.
